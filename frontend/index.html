Historical Document Preservation through Neural Intelligence

Our Mission

Historical documents, personal journals, and ancient archives carry centuries of human wisdom. Yet, fading ink, paper decay, and complex cursive scripts make them nearly impossible for standard digital scanners to decipher.

The AlphaBrothers Digitizer was crafted to resurrect these physical treasures. By pairing deep computer vision with local generative language models, it provides scholars, historians, and archivists with an intelligent, private transcription workstation.

The Multi-Engine Architecture

I
Adaptive Multi-Scale Vision Analyzes images across multiple resolutions to segment faded ink strokes and fine handwriting contours accurately.
II
CRNN Sequence Recognition A Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network trained with Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC) decodes cursive character sequences.
III
Generative AI Reconstruction Ollama (Qwen2.5 7B) performs deterministic linguistic repair, re-gluing broken words and fixing typographical errors while preserving proper nouns.

100% Offline & Private

Confidential manuscripts, legal records, and family heirlooms remain strictly protected. All OCR and LLM inferences execute locally on your machine—no images or transcriptions are ever transmitted to external cloud servers.

Tips for Optimal Transcription

  • Diffuse Illumination: Position light from the sides to avoid glares and heavy paper shadows.
  • Flatten the Page: Minimize page curvature near the spine for the most accurate line segmentation.
  • Parallel Alignment: Orient your camera directly perpendicular to the page surface.
